## Key Challenges & Bottlenecks

### Financial & Fleet Constraints
* **Financial Delays:** Slow disbursement of vendor funds by Accounts leads to outstanding repair bills and vendor friction.
* **Vehicle Aging & Repairs:** Recurrent fleet breakdowns cause high maintenance costs and logistics bottlenecks for field staff.
* **Admin Mobility Deficit:** Insufficient official vehicles assigned specifically to Admin Unit hinders timely task execution.

### HR & Compliance Friction
* **Appraisal Delays:** Late KPI reviews and delayed submissions by HODs stalled SeamlessHR appraisal completion.
* **Low KSS Attendance:** Only 45% participation recorded across Knowledge Sharing Sessions, requiring constant follow-ups.
* **Communication Gaps:** Need for a structured cross-departmental reporting framework to streamline workflows.

---

## Actionable Recommendations

* **Fleet Modernization & Maintenance:** Replace aging, high-maintenance vehicles and institute a mandatory preventive vehicle maintenance schedule. Allocate a dedicated vehicle to Admin logistics.
* **Financial SLA Implementation:** Establish a maximum 48-hour SLA with the Accounts Department for vendor payment approvals to avoid service disruptions.
* **SeamlessHR Appraisal Enforcement:** Lock strict deadlines for KPI submissions on SeamlessHR and mandate HOD accountability to eliminate appraisal extensions.
* **KSS Attendance Mandate:** Enforce mandatory attendance for Knowledge Sharing Sessions with HOD oversight and automated calendar notifications.
* **Manpower Augmentation:** Proceed with approved recruitment of additional office assistants, factory operators, and drivers to maintain production velocity.
